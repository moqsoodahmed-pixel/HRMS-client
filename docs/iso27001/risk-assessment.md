# Information Security Risk Assessment — DutyLaunch HRMS

Document Owner: [SECURITY OWNER] · Approved by: [APPROVER] · Version: 1.0 (Draft) · Last Reviewed: [REVIEW DATE]

## 1. Purpose and scope

This register assesses information-security risk to the DutyLaunch HRMS application, its data, and its supporting infrastructure, in support of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.2 (risk assessment) and Clause 8.2 (risk assessment process). It was compiled by direct code inspection, dependency scanning, and live testing of the application on 2026-08-30, not by a formal organizational risk workshop — [ORGANIZATION NAME] should treat this as a technical starting point and validate/extend it with business stakeholders, not as a substitute for one.

**No residual risk in this register is ever zero.** Every entry that shows strong existing controls still carries a non-zero residual score.

## 2. Methodology

- **Likelihood** (1–5): 1 = rare, 2 = unlikely, 3 = possible, 4 = likely, 5 = almost certain — assessed based on the presence/absence of a real, verified control, not on generic industry assumption.
- **Impact** (1–5): 1 = negligible, 2 = minor, 3 = moderate, 4 = major, 5 = severe — assessed against confidentiality/integrity/availability of HRMS data (identity documents, salary, credentials) and business operation.
- **Risk score** = Likelihood × Impact. Bands: **Low** 1–5, **Medium** 6–11, **High** 12–17, **Critical** 18–25.
- **Inherent risk** = score assuming no application-level controls exist. **Residual risk** = score given the controls actually verified in this codebase today (cited by file path). Where a control is organizational/infrastructural and not yet evidenced, residual risk is scored close to inherent risk — the code cannot mitigate what infrastructure or process hasn't done yet.
- **Status**: `TREATED` (residual risk accepted as adequately controlled for now), `IN PROGRESS` (partial control, more work identified), `OPEN` (no meaningful control yet).

## 4. Summary

- **Critical inherent risks fully treated in this session:** R2 (hardcoded/placeholder JWT secret — was the single most severe finding: a guessable, unrotated fallback signing key).
- **High residual risks still OPEN (require organizational/infrastructure action, not further code changes):** R8 (no backups), R9 (no version control), R16 (no monitoring/SIEM), R18 (security awareness — organizational).
- **High residual risks IN PROGRESS (partial control, follow-up recommended):** R1 (no MFA), R13 (no session revocation on deactivation), R17 (untested DR plan dependent on R8).
- No risk in this register is scored zero residual. The lowest residual scores (R6, R19, R20 at Low) still carry a non-zero score by design.
